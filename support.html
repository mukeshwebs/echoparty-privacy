Need help, found a bug or want to suggest a sound pack? Email us at mukeshyadavwebs@gmail.com. We usually reply within 2 business days.

How do I play?

Tap Quick Play. Everyone in the room hears the same sound, then records an imitation at the same time. Each take is scored on melody, rhythm and attack, and the whole room listens back while the scores roll in. Empty seats are filled with bots, so you can always start a game.

How do I play with friends?

Tap Create, share the 6-letter room code, and your friends enter it with Join. Private rooms work across iPhone and Android.

The game can't hear me

Mimic Party needs microphone access to record your imitation. On iPhone open Settings > Mimic Party and turn on Microphone. On Android open Settings > Apps > Mimic Party > Permissions > Microphone.

I bought something and don't see it

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. Remove Ads and Mimic Plus are restored on every device signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account. Coins are delivered once per purchase.

Someone is being rude

Open the Players list during a match, tap the player and choose Block or Report. Blocked players are muted for you straight away.

Delete my data

Settings > Delete my data removes your profile and recordings instantly. More details: Delete your data.
