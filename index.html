Echo Party - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 October 2026

Echo Party ("the App") is a free party game by SuperBuilds. This policy explains what the App collects and why.

Information we collect

  • An anonymous account ID created by Firebase Authentication. We never ask for your email, phone number or real name.
  • The nickname, avatar, character and country you choose. Other players in your room can see your nickname, avatar, character and country flag.
  • Match recordings. During a round, the short clip you record to imitate a sound is uploaded so the other players in your room can hear it. Clips are linked to that match only. They are deleted when the match ends, or the next time you open the App if a match ended early.
  • Gameplay data such as scores, rounds played and coins, stored on your device and in the match document.
  • Advertising and analytics data collected by Google AdMob and Firebase (for example device type and an advertising ID) to show ads and understand app usage. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK) we ask for your consent first; you can change your choice any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices, and reset or limit your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Reports. If you report a player, we store the report (your account ID, their account ID, their nickname, the reason and the match) so we can review it.

How we use information

To run multiplayer matches, score imitations, keep your progress, show ads and improve the game. We do not sell personal data.

Microphone

The App uses the microphone only while a round is in its recording phase. Audio is scored on your device; only the short take is shared with your room.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

Safety

You can block or report any player from the Players list during a match. Blocked players' recordings are never played to you.

Data retention and deletion

Recordings are deleted after each match. Your profile stays until you delete it: open Settings → Delete my data to permanently remove your profile, progress and account from this device and our servers, or email us and we will delete it within 30 days.
