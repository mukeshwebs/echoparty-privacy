Mimic Party: Voice Challenge - Delete your data

Mimic Party (by SuperBuilds) lets you delete your account and data at any time.

Delete from inside the app (instant)

  1. Open Mimic Party.
  2. Tap the Settings (gear) button on the home screen.
  3. Tap Delete my data, then Delete everything.

This immediately and permanently deletes your player profile (nickname, avatar, character, country), your anonymous account ID, your voice recordings from your latest match, and all progress, coins and settings stored on your device.

Delete by email

If you no longer have the app, email mukeshyadavwebs@gmail.com with the subject "Mimic Party data deletion" and the player ID shown at the bottom of Settings (if you have it). We delete your data within 30 days.

What is kept

Voice recordings are only stored for the match they belong to and are deleted after the match. Abuse reports you sent or that were filed about you may be kept for up to 90 days for safety reviews. Purchases are handled by Google Play and stay in your Google account so they can be restored.
